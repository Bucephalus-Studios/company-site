About Bucephalus Studios


Bucephalus Studios LLC is a software company founded in 2021 by Jeff Stevens. We are dedicated to creating original games free from predatory monetization, low-quality content, and heavy-handed messaging. Games are about fun; let's keep them that way!

The team at Bucephalus Studios consists of a single full-time developer, Jeff Stevens, and a motley crew of freelancers, friends, and family. We're not beholden to publishers or investors, so we're bound to make something absurd and true to ourselves.

We are also an ardent supporter of free and open-source software. Lots of our code, tools, and libraries are available to download and use for free on the "Software" tab of this website and on our Github.
